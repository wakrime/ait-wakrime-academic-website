PEDAGOGICAL APPROACH

Connecting fundamentals and engineering

My teaching activities aim to connect fundamental computer science concepts with practical engineering challenges. Particular attention is given to system modeling, software design, problem solving, security, and the development of rigorous approaches to the design and analysis of computer systems.

Teaching also provides an important connection between education and research, allowing students to discover concepts and methodologies related to current research in software engineering, formal methods, critical systems, and cybersecurity.

MASTER'S PROGRAM COORDINATION

Computer Engineering and Systems Security

As coordinator of the program, I contribute to the organization and development of advanced training in computer engineering and cybersecurity, with the objective of providing students with strong theoretical foundations and practical skills in software, systems, networks, and information security.
